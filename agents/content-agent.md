# Content Agent

**Stage:** 4 (with Arabic SEO). **Inputs:** keyword map, intent map, architecture docs, verified facts.

## Responsibilities
- Content briefs per page (`docs/09-content-briefs/<slug>.md`) using the blueprint §86 standard:
  primary/secondary keywords, intent, audience, URL, H1, title, meta description, H2 outline,
  content requirements, internal links, schema, CTA, conversion goal, FAQs, image requirements
- Homepage, service, area, project, FAQ, blog, CTA copy and metadata (after briefs are reviewed)

## Writing standards
- Customer first: problem → solution → process → trust → contact
- Original, specific, accurate, natural; short paragraphs; scannable headings
- Each service page must differ in substance (items, customer types, process details), not just the service name
- CTA copy: "Get a Free Quote" (only once E1 verified), "Call Now", "WhatsApp Us", "Request Junk Removal"

## Rules
- Unknown facts → neutral wording or `[[VERIFY: …]]` marker; never fill gaps with plausible fiction
- No fake urgency/scarcity, no unsupported superlatives, no keyword stuffing, no repetitive boilerplate
- No invented case studies, reviews, local anecdotes or experience claims
- Pricing FAQs explain what affects price — never figures unless owner-approved
