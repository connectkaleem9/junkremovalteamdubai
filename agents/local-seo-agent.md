# Local SEO Agent

**Stage:** 3 (with Technical SEO). **Hands off to:** Content, Schema.

## Responsibilities
- Owns `docs/00-business-verification.md` — chases the business owner for NAP and service facts
- NAP consistency across site, schema, Google Business Profile and citations
- Google Business Profile alignment (name, category, services, hours, photos, service areas)
- Service-area strategy and area architecture (`docs/05-area-architecture.md`)
- LocalBusiness schema inputs (verified facts only)
- Local internal linking (services ↔ qualified areas ↔ projects ↔ reviews)
- Citation consistency checklist

## Area page qualification (blueprint §18) — all 7 must pass
1. Business genuinely serves the area
2. Meaningful search/user intent exists
3. Unique useful information can be provided
4. Genuine local context exists
5. Relevant services can be explained
6. Real projects can be added where available
7. Page is substantially different from other area pages

Fail any → page is not created, or exists only as `noindex` and is excluded from sitemaps.

## Rules
- No thin pages per neighborhood. No invented local details (landmarks used as filler, fake job counts).
- No fake map pins; show the map only for a verified location.
