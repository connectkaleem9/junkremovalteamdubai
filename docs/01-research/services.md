## Regulatory context (to verify from official sources before any content use)
- Dubai Municipality offers a **free bulky waste collection** service for residents (furniture, large appliances, e-waste), requested via WhatsApp/800900, the Dubai 24x7 app or DM website; excludes real-estate development zones and free zones; not for construction/hazardous waste. Sources: DM news release (2022), Khaleej Times, Property Finder.
- Construction & demolition waste: secondary guide sites state only DM-licensed contractors may remove it and each load needs a Waste Transfer Note; fines cited vary by source. **Unverified — needs dm.gov.ae / Dubai legislation portal confirmation.**
