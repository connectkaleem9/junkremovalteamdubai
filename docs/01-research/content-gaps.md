# Content Gaps & Opportunities

**Date:** 2026-09-16. Each opportunity states *why a real Dubai customer would find it useful* (blueprint §54).

| # | Opportunity | Gap observed | Customer value | Page type | Depends on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Honest pricing explainer: what determines junk removal cost in Dubai (volume, item type, floor/access, disposal, timing) | Competitors either hide pricing or publish unverifiable AED ranges | Sets expectations, reduces hidden-fee fear | Guide + FAQ section on every service page | E2 (no figures unless owner approves) |
| G2 | "Dubai Municipality free bulky waste service vs. hiring a junk removal company" | Only junk-company posts that bury the free option | Honest help → trust; ranks for informational DM queries | Guide | Official DM source verification |
| G3 | Move-out clearance checklist for Dubai apartments (service lift booking, building management notice, handover timing) | Generic content, rarely Dubai-specific | Directly tied to highest-urgency customers | Guide + apartment clearance page section | Owner's real experience; community rules vary — avoid stating specific building rules as universal |
| G4 | What we take / what we don't — and where to take what we don't (batteries, paint, gas cylinders, e-waste) | Competitors list refusals but offer no next step | Solves the customer's whole problem | Page section + guide | D1, D2 |
| G5 | Construction & renovation waste: what the law requires and how to check a contractor is licensed | EMD sites say "licensed experts" without evidence | Protects customers from fines | Guide (even if we don't offer the service) | Official sources |
| G6 | Real project case studies with before/after, area, date, service | Competitors use stock photos and invented stats | Proof | Project pages | G1–G4 from owner (§G) |
| G7 | Office clearance planning guide (inventory, IT/data-bearing equipment, after-hours access, lease handover) | Thin B2B pages | B2B decision support | Guide + office page | Owner's actual process |
| G8 | Genuine Arabic content for clearance/removal intent | Arabic SERP dominated by used-furniture buyers | Serves Arabic speakers who need removal, not a sale | `/ar/` service pages | Native reviewer (H6) |
| G9 | Villa clearance specifics (majlis, garden, storage rooms, large volumes, multi-truck jobs) | Villa content mostly templated | Realistic planning | Villa page | Owner capability |
| G10 | Clear business identity page (who, where, how we work, photos of real team/truck) | Most competitors anonymous | Trust / Ads landing-page requirement | About page | B1–B10, G2 |

## Explicitly NOT pursuing
- Area+service doorway pages (e.g. "junk removal in [every community]") — competitors' pattern, violates rules 23–24.
- Invented cost tables, tonnage stats, recycling percentages, customer counts.
