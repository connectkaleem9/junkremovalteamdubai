## Intent rules for page design
- Transactional pages: CTA visible without scrolling on mobile; contact options repeated after services, process, and before FAQ (blueprint §67).
- Informational pages: answer first, CTA secondary; no aggressive popups.
- Every service page states what we **don't** do as clearly as what we do — reduces unqualified leads and supports Ads relevance.
